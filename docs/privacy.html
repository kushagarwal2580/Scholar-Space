Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

Scholar Space is built with a strict privacy-first, local-first architecture. This Privacy Policy clearly describes how our Android application operates, what information is involved, and how your privacy is guaranteed.

1. Zero Developer Access (No Backend Servers)

We do not own, operate, or maintain any backend servers or databases. As the developers, we have zero access to your device, your files, your Google Drive, or any data you create or manage within Scholar Space:

  • We cannot view, read, modify, track, or access any of your documents, notes, schedules, flashcards, recordings, or study materials.
  • We cannot see how many files you store, their names, file sizes, or your storage usage.
  • All data processing and storage occurs exclusively on your local device or directly between your installed app and your own personal Google Drive account.

2. Information and Authentication

  • Local Device Storage: Your notes, library files, timetable schedules, stopwatch and timer sessions, bookmarks, and user preferences are stored exclusively on your device's local storage (via local database and files).
  • Google Authentication (Google Sign-In): When you choose to sign in, the authentication is handled securely on-device by Google Identity Services. The app receives basic authentication information (such as your Google account email and display name) solely within the app on your phone to display your account profile and connect to your Google Drive. This information is never transmitted to us or any third-party server.
  • Google Drive Backup & Sync: If you connect Google Drive, the app directly interfaces with Google Drive API from your device using the restricted drive.file scope. This grants the app client permission to create and sync files only inside an app-created folder in your own personal Google Drive. Neither the developers nor any intermediary service can view, read, inspect, or manage any files or folders in your Google Drive.

3. Device Permissions & Hardware Usage

Scholar Space only requests device permissions necessary for features you actively use, handled entirely on your device:

  • Notifications & Foreground Services: Used to display live stopwatch/timer progress, alarm reminders, and persistent timer controls in your status bar. No notification data leaves your phone.
  • Audio Recording: Used solely if you record voice memos or audio notes within your study library. Audio is saved only to your local device.
  • Exact Alarms: Used to deliver timely schedule alerts and study reminders at user-configured times.

4. Data Retention, Control, and Deletion

Because you retain 100% ownership and custody of your data:

  • Local Data: You can completely erase your data at any time by clearing the app's cache and storage in Android Settings, or by uninstalling the application.
  • Google Drive Cloud Data: You can view, manage, download, or delete your backup files directly in your Google Drive at any time. To remove all cloud backups, simply delete the "Scholar Space" folder from your Google Drive, or revoke the app's access in your Google Account Security Settings.

5. Third-Party Services

The app directly integrates with standard Google Play services (Google Identity Services and Google Drive API). Their handling of authentication and cloud storage on Google's infrastructure is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as new app features are introduced. The updated version will always be indicated by the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.
