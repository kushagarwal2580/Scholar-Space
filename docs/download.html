Features

Application Purpose

Scholar Space is an application designed to help students manage their study sessions, files, and notes. Its primary purpose for obtaining OAuth access is to allow users to securely backup and synchronize their local study data, schedules, and materials across multiple devices using their own personal Google Drive storage.

Cloud Synchronization

Securely backup and sync your library, profile, and app state to Google Drive to seamlessly switch between devices.

Timers & Stopwatches

Track your study sessions accurately. Includes persistent background notifications so you always know your progress.

Material Library

Keep your study materials organized. Easily manage course notes, textbooks, and audio recordings.

Built-in File Viewer

View your PDFs and listen to audio recordings directly within the app without needing third-party viewers.

Profile Management

Customize your student profile with notes, statuses, and personal study goals.

Smart Reminders

Set persistent reminders and day counters for your exams. Stay completely organized and ahead of any tight deadlines without stress.

Offline Support

Access your notes and materials fully offline. The app functions optimally without needing an internet connection.

Document Scanner

Easily digitize your physical study materials with the built-in ML document scanner directly into your library.

Support & Links
