Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Scholar Space collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our Android application.

1. Information We Collect

Scholar Space is designed to be a privacy-first application. We do not run any independent backend servers to collect or sell your personal data.

  • Local Data: By default, all your notes, schedules, files, and study data are stored locally on your device.
  • Google Drive Data (Optional): If you choose to enable Cloud Synchronization, you sign in with your Google Account. We request access strictly to a dedicated, application-specific folder in your Google Drive (via the drive.file scope) to securely backup and sync your devices.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Your Google Drive data is used solely for backing up and restoring your own app library between your devices.
  • We do not have access to view, read, or modify any other files in your Google Drive outside of the ones created by Scholar Space.
  • We do not share any data with third parties.

3. Data Retention and Deletion

You have full control over your data. Because your data is stored on your device and inside your own Google Drive:

  • You can delete your data by clearing the app's local storage or uninstalling the app.
  • You can delete your cloud backups at any time directly through your Google Drive interface or by revoking Scholar Space's access in your Google Account settings.

4. Third-Party Services

The app uses Google Identity Services for authentication and Google Drive API for backups. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for information on how Google handles your authentication data.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be indicated by the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us by creating an issue on our GitHub repository.
